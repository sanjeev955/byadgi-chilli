About Byadgi Chilli

Byadgi chilli is a famous variety from Karnataka, India, known for its deep red color, rich aroma, and moderate pungency. It is widely used in Indian cuisine, spice powders, and food processing industries.

Accurate quality grading helps farmers get fair market value, supports traders in maintaining standards, and improves consistency in export and domestic supply chains.
